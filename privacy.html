Privacy Policy

How Frezix may collect, use, and handle information.

Effective date: October 10, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Frezix, developed by Febreze Gamerz, may process information when you use the bot. We aim to collect only information needed to operate its features and provide server functionality.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on which features a Discord server uses, Frezix may process the following information:

  • Discord identifiers: User IDs and server (guild) IDs used to identify users and servers.
  • Invite tracking: Invite codes, invite usage information, inviter IDs, and invitee IDs needed to provide invite statistics and leaderboards.
  • Server configuration: Settings for welcome and farewell messages, autoroles, temporary roles, prefixes, and other enabled features.
  • Command information: Information needed to respond to commands, such as command options and the IDs of relevant users or channels.
  • Message content: Content or text supplied through commands or configured messages may be processed when needed to perform a requested feature.

The exact information handled depends on the commands and features enabled in your server.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide utility, moderation, role management, and server information commands.
  • Track invitations and calculate invite statistics.
  • Send configured welcome or farewell messages.
  • Apply server settings and manage temporary roles.
  • Maintain the bot's security, troubleshoot errors, and prevent abuse.

4. Storage and Retention

Some settings and invite tracking information may be retained so features continue to work between commands or sessions. Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant feature or operational purpose.

We do not promise that every type of information is stored, logged, or deleted in the same way. Retention depends on the feature and the data involved.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed through Discord to operate the bot, or disclosed where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect the bot, its users, or others from abuse or security threats.

Discord handles information under its own privacy policy and terms. We do not control Discord's data practices.

6. Permissions and Server Administrators

Server administrators control which people can use many of Frezix's features and what settings are configured. If you use a server that has Frezix installed, its administrators may be able to see information that Discord makes available to them or that is displayed by bot commands.

7. Your Choices and Data Requests

You can stop interacting with Frezix or ask a server administrator to remove the bot from a server. If you have a question about information associated with your Discord ID, or want to request access to or deletion of information, contact us through our support Discord server.

We may need enough information to identify the relevant data and verify the request. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

Frezix is intended for people who are permitted to use Discord under Discord's applicable age requirements. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe information has been provided inappropriately, contact us through our support server.

9. Security

Reasonable measures should be taken to protect information handled by the bot. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security, and we cannot promise that unauthorized access or data loss will never occur.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when Frezix's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Please review it periodically.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact us through the official Frezix support Discord server.
