Your information matters

Privacy Policy.

Transparency matters. This policy explains what information Frezix may process, why it may be needed and what choices are available to you.

◷ Effective date: October 10, 2026

01 Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Frezix, developed by Febreze Gamerz, may process information when you use the bot.

We aim to handle information only as needed to operate the bot's features, maintain functionality and provide server-related services.

02 Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled in a Discord server and the commands used, Frezix may process the following categories of information:

  • Discord identifiers: User IDs, server IDs and relevant channel IDs used to identify users, servers and destinations for bot actions.
  • Invite tracking information: Invite codes, invite usage counts, inviter IDs and invitee IDs used for invite statistics and leaderboards.
  • Server configuration: Settings for welcome and farewell messages, autoroles, temporary roles, prefixes and other enabled features.
  • Command information: Command names, options and information required to complete a requested action.
  • Message content: Text submitted through commands or configured messages may be processed when necessary to provide the requested feature.

The information handled depends on which features are used. We do not claim that every listed category is collected in every server.

03 How Information Is Used

Information may be used for purposes such as:

  • Providing utility, moderation, role management and server information commands.
  • Tracking invitations and calculating invite statistics.
  • Sending configured welcome and farewell messages.
  • Applying server settings and managing temporary roles.
  • Responding to commands and maintaining bot functionality.
  • Troubleshooting technical issues and helping prevent abuse.

Information should only be used for purposes related to operating, maintaining and improving Frezix's features.

04 Storage and Retention

Some configuration settings and invite tracking information may be stored so that features can continue working between commands or sessions.

Retention depends on the feature and how the bot is implemented. We aim to retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant functionality, security or operational purpose.

We cannot promise a specific deletion schedule for every category of information.

05 Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information.

Frezix interacts with Discord to provide its features. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the bot or others from abuse or security threats.

Discord processes information under its own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. We do not control Discord's independent data practices.

06 Permissions and Server Administrators

Server administrators control many of the settings and permissions used by Frezix.

Depending on their permissions and the bot's features, administrators and moderators may be able to see information made available by Discord or displayed through bot commands.

Server administrators are responsible for how they configure the bot and use information within their communities.

07 Your Choices and Data Requests

You can stop using Frezix or ask a server administrator to remove the bot from a server.

If you have a question about information associated with your Discord ID, or want to request access to or deletion of information, contact us through the official Frezix support Discord server.

We may need sufficient information to identify relevant records and verify a request. Some information may need to be retained for legal, security or legitimate operational reasons.

08 Children's Privacy

Frezix is intended for people who are permitted to use Discord under Discord's applicable age requirements.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe information has been provided inappropriately, please contact us through our support server.

09 Security

Reasonable measures should be taken to protect information handled by the bot.

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security. We cannot guarantee that unauthorized access, service interruptions or data loss will never occur.

10 Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when Frezix's features or data practices change.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Please review this page periodically to stay informed about how information may be handled.

11 Contact

Have a privacy question or want to make a data request?

Contact us through the official Frezix support Discord server.
