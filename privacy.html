Privacy Policy

How Frezix may collect, use and handle information.

Effective date: October 10, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Frezix, developed by Febreze Gamerz, may process information when you use the bot. We aim to handle information only as needed to operate its features and provide server functionality.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled in a Discord server, Frezix may process:

  • Discord identifiers: User IDs, server IDs and relevant channel IDs used to identify users, servers and destinations for bot actions.
  • Invite tracking information: Invite codes, invite usage counts, inviter IDs and invitee IDs used for invite statistics and leaderboards.
  • Server configuration: Settings for welcome and farewell messages, autoroles, temporary roles, prefixes and other enabled features.
  • Command information: Command options and information needed to complete a requested action.
  • Message content: Text submitted through commands or configured messages may be processed when needed to provide the requested feature.

The information handled depends on which commands and features are used. We do not claim that every listed type is collected in every server.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide utility, moderation, role management and server information commands.
  • Track invitations and calculate invite statistics.
  • Send configured welcome and farewell messages.
  • Apply server settings and manage temporary roles.
  • Respond to commands, maintain functionality, troubleshoot problems and help prevent abuse.

4. Storage and Retention

Some settings and invite tracking information may be retained so features can continue to work between commands or sessions. Retention depends on the feature and how the bot is implemented.

We aim to retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant functionality, security or operational purpose. We cannot promise a specific deletion schedule for every category of information.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. Frezix interacts with Discord to provide bot features. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the bot or others from abuse or security threats.

Discord handles information under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not control Discord's data practices.

6. Permissions and Server Administrators

Server administrators control many of the settings and permissions used by Frezix. Administrators and moderators may be able to see information made available by Discord or displayed through bot commands. They are responsible for how they use that information within their server.

7. Your Choices and Data Requests

You can stop using Frezix or ask a server administrator to remove the bot from a server. To ask a question about information associated with your Discord ID or request access to or deletion of information, contact us through the official Frezix support server.

We may need enough information to identify the relevant records and verify a request. Some information may need to be retained for legal, security or legitimate operational reasons.

8. Children's Privacy

Frezix is intended for people who are permitted to use Discord under Discord's applicable age requirements. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe information has been provided inappropriately, contact us through our support server.

9. Security

Reasonable measures should be taken to protect information handled by the bot. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security, and we cannot guarantee that unauthorized access, service interruptions or data loss will never occur.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when Frezix's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Please review it periodically.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact us through the official Frezix support Discord server.
